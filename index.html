Public Project Showcase

Chain of Happiness

A social-impact platform and research project exploring how needs, resources, and acts of help can be coordinated more effectively through digital infrastructure and System Dynamics.

Visit live platform →GitHub showcase
Platform

Make help easier to coordinate.

The platform is designed around structured requests and offers, with support extending beyond money to goods, services, knowledge, time, and other useful resources.

Research

Study the feedback behind participation.

Chain of Happiness is also a System Dynamics research project examining cooperation, helping behavior, participation, matching, and the growth dynamics of a social-support system.

Product logic

Core concepts

Requests & offers

Represent needs and available resources explicitly so they can be matched and coordinated.

Non-monetary exchange

Support help through time, skills, services, goods, or knowledge—not only financial contributions.

Integrity rules

Use platform rules and workflow design to preserve trust and discourage misuse of the exchange process.

Matching

Reduce coordination friction between people who need something and people who can provide it.

Research feedback

Use empirical and modeling work to inform how the platform evolves.

Scalable coordination

Explore how a small helping interaction can become part of a broader, repeatable social-support structure.

Academic work

System Dynamics research

The Chain of Happiness research was presented at the 2026 International System Dynamics Conference (ISDC) in Delft and is being developed further for academic publication.
